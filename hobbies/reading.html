About

Reading has always been difficult for me — I have dyslexia and dysgraphia, plus ADHD, so I can't even sit still in the first place. But I went to a school that highly valued reading, Atlanta Classical Academy, and I found a love for reading and literature through it.

I had a little mini library of a couple hundred books. I love philosophy and a good literature book — the good, hardcore classics.
