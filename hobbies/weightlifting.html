Weightlifting 🏋️

Started in football, kept going.

About

I started weightlifting in football and I enjoyed it a lot, surprisingly — I thought I would never like to lift or be an exercise guy. I kept going with it during football and after it.

It's fun to get my energy out in a way where I can push and shove things instead of just running, which I've never liked — it doesn't get the same energy out. Weightlifting is more fun for me.

The numbers

I think my maxes are somewhere on this website. Oh — right here (all in pounds):

  • 🏋️ Squat: 475–490 lbs
  • 💪 Bench: ~260 lbs
  • ⚡ Power Clean: 235 lbs
