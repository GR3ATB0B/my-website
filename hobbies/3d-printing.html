About

I mainly 3D print for project purposes. I never really liked printing knick-knacks — I've always loved utilitarian, purposeful prints.

The printers

I run a Bambu A1 and I'm looking to get the AMS for it. Before that I had an Elegoo Neptune 3 — took it apart, modded it, and upgraded to the A1 later down the road. The Neptune taught me a lot about layer lines, slicing, and the technical stuff that goes along with it.

With the A1 you literally press a button and it prints, which is nice — but I kind of miss the troubleshooting. It made it more fun.
