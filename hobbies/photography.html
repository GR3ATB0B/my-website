About

I started on my mom's camera — a Nikon DSLR — then moved to film. I got my grandpa's 35mm Pentax and started shooting a lot of film.

One day at Goodwill I came across 20 rolls of 120 medium-format Kodak film — the whole package was worth about $400. I've been shooting on it ever since. I love shooting moments and finding moments.
