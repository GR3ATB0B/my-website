Spanish & Languages 🇪🇸

Failed Latin, loved Spanish.

About

We took Spanish in elementary school — I was crap at it, but I had this really great teacher we called "Profe" who taught my whole class really well and got us all loving Spanish. Then middle school was Latin, and I was awful at that. I think I failed every single year — and it was a high school class we took in middle school, so it's on my transcript. Freshman year you could choose, so I switched back to Spanish.

I also worked at Chick-fil-A for a while, and a lot of the back-of-house crew was Hispanic, so I'd talk to them in Spanish to get practice. Spanish feels very logical to me — systems and equations, like coding and math. If I want to say this, then this, and then that. I'm dyslexic, and for some reason it clicked really well. I learned a lot about how I learn.
