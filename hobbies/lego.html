About

I've been into Legos ever since I was born — some of my first birthday and Christmas presents were Legos, and they just kept coming. It was the first way I figured out that I liked making stuff.

Still to this day, I'll spend my paychecks on Lego sets or save up for one. I've always loved the mechanical ones — the ones that move and do stuff — but I like the stationary ones too: the Wall-E's, the movie ones, the Star Wars ships.
