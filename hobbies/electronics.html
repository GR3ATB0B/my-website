About

I love building electronics — this is where most of my projects fall. Mostly ESP32s and Seeed Studio XIAO minis; they're really cool development boards and I use them a lot. Component soldering is just a fun skill to have. Hooking up sensors, making things do things, and coding it too.

A lot of it feeds into my projects, like the House Point Counter and the Ares II ionic thruster.
