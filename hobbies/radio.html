About

Radio is a newfound hobby. I have my ham license and my GMRS license, and I like listening in — like truckers on the CB radio in my truck when I drove from Atlanta over to Auburn.

It's just cool that all these waves are floating around you right now, and you can tune in and open them up. Cool science stuff.
