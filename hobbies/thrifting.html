Goodwill Hunting 🛍️

Where all the other hobbies started.

About

Thrifting is what got me into all of these random hobbies. Go to Goodwill, buy some cheap crap like a toaster, take it apart to figure out how it works, then use those parts — and the new info — to make something cooler. Like a toaster gun.

If I had to give advice to anyone trying to get into this stuff: go to Goodwill and find something to hack or make cooler. Let your mind go wild.
