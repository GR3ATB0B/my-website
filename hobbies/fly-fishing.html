Calming, and you make your own flies.

About

An up-and-coming hobby I've been really into lately. It's a lot like camping — and even flower making, because you have to make your own flies. It's a very calming hobby.

It feels more sophisticated than normal fishing, a little more skilled, which I like. Honestly, it's actually easier than normal casting. And you can catch some yummy fish.
